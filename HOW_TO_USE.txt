HOW TO USE: chord-progression-recommender
==================================================

PURPOSE
  Mines 31,032 real-world song chord sequences from the Lakh MIDI dataset on
  Drive B to recommend mathematically and musically proven chord transitions,
  passing chords, and full progressions backed by statistical song analysis.

CAPABILITIES
  - Scans 31k songs to find the most frequent chords that follow any given chord
  - Categorizes suggestions into Strong Resolutions, Modal Colors, and Surprise Turnarounds
  - Provides Roman numeral functional analysis and voice-leading explanations
  - Breaks down specific keyboard/guitar notes and chord spellings for every recommended chord
  - Cites real famous songs from the database that feature the exact chord movement
  - Discovers full songs matching an input multi-chord sequence (e.g., Am -> F -> C -> G)
  - Connects directly with ableton-midi-chord-builder for 1-click MIDI progression clip export

USE CASES
  - You have a starting chord or two and are stuck on what chord should come next
  - You want fresh harmonic ideas for a verse, chorus, bridge, or intro
  - You want to find real-world songs that share a specific chord progression
  - You need to break out of routine 4-chord loops with modal interchange or secondary dominants
  - You want to verify whether a chord movement has strong empirical precedent in hit music

IDEAS
  - Use with reharmonization-engine to upgrade a pop progression with jazz or neo-soul chords
  - Search for your favorite obscure chord sequence to see which artists wrote songs with it
  - Generate an Ableton MIDI clip directly from the top recommended progression
  - Discover era-specific harmonic trends by pairing with song-era-harmony-analyzer
  - Build tension in pre-choruses by finding the most dramatic surprise turnaround chords

PROMPTS YOU CAN PASTE
  I'm writing a verse in D minor starting on Dm to Gm. What chord should come next?

  What chords go well after Cmaj7?

  Recommend a chord progression for a melancholic R&B track in F minor.

  Find songs in our database that use the progression Am - F - C - G.

  What are some unexpected turnaround chords for a bridge after Em7?

EXAMPLE FLOW
  You:   I have chords Dm to Gm. What should come next?
  Model: [Mines 31,032 songs in milliseconds]
         Top recommendations:
         1. C (Classic ii - V - I resolution, 28% of transitions)
            - Example: Daniel Balavoine, "Sauver L'Amour"
            - Voicing: C - E - G (Root position) or C - G - C - E (Open piano)
         2. Bb (Dramatic bVI deceptive lift, 18%)
            - Example: Verona, "La Musica"
         3. Eb7#9 (Spicy tritone passing turnaround, 9%)
         [Provides chord notes, Roman numerals, and voice-leading analysis]

DEFAULT GOALS IF YOU DON'T NAME ONE
  1. Parse the starting chord or chord sequence from your prompt.
  2. Query `recommend_chords.py` to calculate the top empirical harmonic transitions.
  3. Categorize next steps into Strong Resolution, Modal/Deceptive Color, and Surprise Turnaround.
  4. Provide Roman numeral analysis, note spelling/voicings, and 2 real song references.
  5. Offer to export the selected progression into a MIDI clip for Ableton Live.
